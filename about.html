About Me

Hello! My name is Jeremiah Funck, and I am a college student with interests in communications, multimedia production, photography, marketing, and digital content creation.

Since 2019, I have developed experience in photography, beginning with nature and landscape work and later expanding into portrait photography, community headshot sessions, and creative multimedia projects. Through these experiences, I have built skills in visual storytelling, client communication, project coordination, and digital media production.

In addition to photography, I maintain a personal portfolio website and continue exploring opportunities in communications, public relations, marketing, and business-related fields. I enjoy creating projects that combine creativity, technology, and effective communication.

My goal is to continue developing professionally through academic study, hands-on projects, internships, and collaborative opportunities while building a portfolio that demonstrates both creative and communication skills.
